# Washington State EV Analysis

Is Washington's public charging network keeping pace with EV growth, and how are income and rurality associated with where the gaps are largest?

**Live page:** https://sravyasss.github.io/Washington-State-EV-Analysis/

## What is in this repo
- `index.html` is our Homework 8 notebook (data sources, cleaning, exploration, and visualization brainstorm)
- `WA_EV_Project.zip` has the datasets we used

## Data sources
- WA Department of Licensing, EV Population Size History by County
- U.S. Department of Energy, Alternative Fuels Data Center, charging stations
- U.S. Census Bureau, median household income (ACS B19013) and county population estimates and boundaries
- USDA, Rural-Urban Continuum Codes (2023)
- WA Department of Licensing, EV Population Data
